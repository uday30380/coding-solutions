## Problem

### Challenge - Two Video Embeds

For this task, you'll create a single webpage with two different YouTube videos. Each video will serve a different purpose, and you'll use URL parameters to achieve the desired effect.

 **Instructions:** 

- Embed Your First Video: Choose any YouTube video you like. Embed it on your page as a standard, regular video player. This video should have all the default controls (play/pause, volume, fullscreen).
- Embed Your Second Video: Choose the same video. Embed it to serve as a background video effect. Modify its src URL to do the following: Make it start playing automatically when the page loads. Make it play silently (no sound). Make it loop continuously. Hide the default player controls.
